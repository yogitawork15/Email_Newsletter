## Overview

This repository contains the complete email newsletter content created for **FreeInternships.in** as part of the Free Digital Marketing Internship. The goal was to write engaging, conversion-focused newsletter copy — from subject lines to full email samples — that builds relationships with subscribers and drives them to start the internship.

## Tools Used

- MailChimp (email platform / send & A/B test)
- Google Docs (drafting & collaboration)
- Canva (visual design for email header/footer)
- Grammarly (grammar & readability check)

## Methodology

1. Studied email newsletter best practices and successful campaign examples
2. Defined target audience (students and early-career professionals seeking internships) and newsletter goals
3. Drafted 10 subject line variations across different psychological triggers
4. Wrote 3 full newsletter samples following a consistent 5-part structure (Subject → Header/Greeting → Body → CTA → Footer)
5. Compiled a keyword and phrase glossary to keep tone and messaging consistent across future newsletters
6. Proofread all copy with Grammarly for grammar and readability

## Outcome

Three ready-to-send newsletter variations and ten tested subject lines were produced, giving FreeInternships.in a ready email content library that can be loaded into MailChimp and scheduled immediately.
